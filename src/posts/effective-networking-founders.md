For startup founders, a strong network can be a superpower, unlocking access to funding, talent, partnerships, and invaluable advice. But effective networking isn't about schmoozing or collecting LinkedIn connections; it's about building authentic, mutually beneficial relationships.

### Give Before You Get

Approach networking with a mindset of generosity. How can you help others in your network? Offer introductions, share relevant resources, or provide feedback. Building goodwill is the foundation of strong relationships.

### Quality Over Quantity

Focus on building deeper connections with a smaller group of relevant people rather than superficially engaging with hundreds. Identify individuals who align with your goals and industry, and invest time in nurturing those relationships.

### Be Prepared and Specific

When attending events or reaching out online, have a clear understanding of what you're looking for and be able to articulate your startup's mission concisely. If asking for help, be specific about what you need.

### Follow Up Authentically

After meeting someone, follow up with a personalized message referencing your conversation. Avoid generic templates. Suggest a concrete next step if appropriate, like a brief virtual coffee or sharing a relevant article.
