The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) is a cornerstone of the Lean Startup methodology. It's not about launching a buggy, unfinished product; it's about launching the simplest version that delivers core value to early adopters and allows you to gather crucial feedback.

### Why Build an MVP?

*   **Validate Assumptions:** Test your core hypotheses about the problem and solution with real users before investing significant resources.
*   **Faster Time-to-Market:** Get your product in front of users sooner and start the learning cycle.
*   **Reduce Development Waste:** Avoid building features nobody wants by focusing on the essential core functionality first.
*   **Gather Feedback:** Collect invaluable user insights to inform future iterations and product direction.

### Defining Your MVP

Focus ruthlessly on the 'minimum'. What is the absolute core problem you are solving, and what is the simplest feature set required to solve it for your target early adopters? Resist the urge to add 'nice-to-haves'.

### Building and Launching

Choose the right technology and approach for speed and flexibility. The goal isn't perfection, but a functional product that allows for testing and learning. Plan your launch strategy to reach your target early adopters effectively.
